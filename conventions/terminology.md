# Terminology

Names and wording for department materials.

## Institution and programs

- The university is "CSU East Bay," never "Cal State East Bay." "Cal State Apply" keeps its product name.
- The department is the Department of Music and Performing Arts.
- The certificate is the Certificate in Music Education.
- FAST is the Falcon Accelerated Success Track.
- The M.A. emphases are Performance (Classical or Jazz), Choral Conducting, and Composition. The label "Performance (Classical or Jazz)" stays as written in M.A. materials.
- Office of Graduate Studies links use csueastbay.edu/graduate-studies/.

## Courses and rotation

- MUS 661–668 is "Graduate Applied Study" everywhere.
- Rotation labels take an uppercase letter: fall A, spring A, fall B, spring B.

## FAST

- The Music FAST application is an "application form," never a "packet."
- FAST blended graduate coursework replaces "undergraduate electives," not music electives or free electives.
- On the FAST roadmaps, the graduate-year ensemble is a single "Ensemble(s)" row at 2 units, never a "second ensemble" row.

## Applied study

- Adding a second applied area "requires a placement audition." That wording stays.
