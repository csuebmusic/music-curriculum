# Workflow

Working rules for this repository. Prose style, register, and feedback follow the "how I want you to communicate" section of Inés Thiebaut's profile.

## Scope

The repository holds Inés Thiebaut's non-course work at CSU East Bay: curriculum and committee work, university documents, assessment, and webmaster work for the Department of Music and Performing Arts and the College of Letters, Arts, and Social Sciences. The course projects are separate. Each folder's README lists its files.

## Source of truth

- The files in `conventions/` are authoritative. Where an earlier instruction or conversation disagrees with them, they govern, and the discrepancy is flagged for reconciliation.
- The repository governs over the live Cascade site and other copies.
- When a decision changes, the file that holds it changes with it.

## Before drafting

Confirm the workstream, the audience, the output channel (a Word or PDF document, house-styled HTML, or Cascade markup), and any length or format constraint. Short requests are inferred.

## Forms

A university form follows its own structure and required fields.

## Git

- Commits go directly to `main`, one logical change per commit.
- The author is Inés Thiebaut <ines.thiebaut@csueastbay.edu>.
- A commit message names what changed.
- Claude pushes after each commit when the session allows it. Otherwise Claude commits in Inés's local clone and Inés pushes.
- Reorganizations, mass renames, and deletions are set out as a plan and wait for approval.

## Kept out of the repository

- Student names, student work, and grades.
- Graduate student checklists and lists holding personal information.
- The CCC review workbook, `CLASS-Curriculum-2026-2027.xlsx`.
- Access tokens.
