The Office of Graduate Studies supports prospective and current graduate students at Cal State East Bay, from admissions guidance through research, professional development, and career planning.

Are you interested in applying to a graduate program? Complete a request for information form.

Programs

Cal State East Bay offers more than fifty graduate programs: master’s and doctoral degrees, credentials, and certificates. Program pages, coordinator contacts, terms open for application, and program-specific deadlines are on the graduate program coordinators page.

Applying takes two steps: an application through Cal State Apply, then the additional materials your department asks for. The prospective graduate students page covers both, along with admission requirements, cost, and information for international applicants.

Falcon Accelerated Success Track (FAST) programs

FAST programs merge a bachelor’s degree and a master’s degree in a related discipline into one pathway. East Bay undergraduates with 60 to 90 units can start the master’s program early, take graduate courses that count toward both degrees, and pay undergraduate tuition for those courses. Twenty-seven pathways across all four colleges are listed on the FAST programs page.

Events

The Office of Graduate Studies holds workshops, information sessions, and the Grad Slam research presentation competition through the year. Dates are on the graduate events page.
