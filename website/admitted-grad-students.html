Welcome to graduate school at Cal State East Bay.

Next steps

The Office of Admissions covers what to do once you’re admitted: setting up your NetID and email account, getting into MyCSUEB, and registering for courses. Start with the next steps for admitted students.

International students have a separate set of steps from the International Admissions Office, on their admitted students page, along with the International Student Handbook.

Orientation

The university and your department both provide orientation materials covering your degree program and the courses to enroll in.

Student Outreach holds an online orientation for new graduate students each semester, and a recording is available. The Office of Graduate Studies has an additional video orientation covering further topics.

Your department offers a program-specific orientation. Contact your academic department or your program’s coordinator, listed on the graduate program coordinators page.

The International Admissions Office runs an orientation for international students; details are on their orientation page.

Advising

For questions about your program, contact your program’s coordinator on the graduate program coordinators page or your academic department. Some programs have further advising resources: the Office of CBE Graduate Advising for business programs and the Credential Student Service Center for credential programs.

University Catalog

The University Catalog describes your program and the policies governing all graduate students at Cal State East Bay. The graduate student handbook covers university requirements, policies, and procedures.

Accessibility

Any qualified individual with a disability may request accommodations. Contact Accessibility Services.
