Cal State East Bay offers a range of financial support for graduate students. The Office of Graduate Studies coordinates the fellowships and programs in the sections below. The Financial Aid Office handles grants, loans, and work study, and its page for graduate students collects what applies at this level.

Start with the Free Application for Federal Student Aid (FAFSA) or the California Dream Act Application (CADAA). Both are free and both are filed annually. Eligibility for most of the support on this page depends on having one on file, including the Graduate Equity Fellowship below.

Tuition and fees are on the cost of attendance page. Scroll the tuition chart horizontally to reach the graduate tuition tab.

The State University Grant pays the mandatory state university tuition fee for California residents enrolled at least half time, which is 4 units in a master’s program and 6 in a credential program. Awards are based on need and annual funding, with priority for students who apply by March 2. Students whose registration fees are paid from another source, including fee scholarships, fee waivers, Cal Grant fees, and Vocational Rehabilitation, aren’t eligible, and the Office of Financial Aid needs to know when a third party pays registration fees.

The Graduate Business Grant may be available to students in the M.B.A. or M.S. Business Analytics programs who are paying the professional program fee. The amount varies with federal unmet need and CSU requirements. Filing the FAFSA or CADAA puts you in consideration automatically, with no separate application; eligible students are notified each semester after the term’s census date. These and the Teacher Education Assistance for College and Higher Education (TEACH) Grant are described on the grants page.

Graduate students can borrow through federal direct loans, Graduate PLUS loans, and private loans.

Federal Work Study pays for part-time work while you’re enrolled, and eligibility comes from the FAFSA or CADAA. Campus jobs outside work study, including student assistant and teaching associate positions, are posted through Human Resources.
