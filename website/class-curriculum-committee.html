Guiding principles

The CLASS Curriculum Committee is committed to collegiality, diversity, equity, and inclusion in all policies and procedures. Thus, our guiding principles ensure that all written and oral communications, virtually and in person, are cordial and professional, meaning we respect similar and differing opinions and therefore cannot accept language or behavior that is contrary to those principles. We thank you in advance for abiding by this commitment in conversations, whether as a committee member or as a guest at committee proceedings; any such deviation from that commitment will not be tolerated, and is grounds for excusal from said conversation. Behavior or language resulting in an excusal may require a follow-up meeting with the Associate Dean and Committee Chair prior to continued committee meetings or discussions.

Membership

The committee has at least five and no more than seven members: the CLASS associate dean and, where possible, one probationary or tenured faculty member from each of the departments and programs representing the arts, humanities and letters, social sciences, interdisciplinary programs, and professional programs, as selected by each group’s department and program chairs. Members serve two-year terms. Where a constituency has no faculty member available, the seat may be filled by a faculty member from another constituency, or left vacant for the term.
