Graduate Student Ambassadors represent the graduate community at Cal State East Bay. They come from across the academic disciplines and share their own experience with prospective and current graduate students through mentorship, outreach, and events. If you’re proud of your work in your graduate program and want to share that with the campus, you’d make a good ambassador.

Jump to what ambassadors do, benefits, eligibility, how to apply, current ambassadors, past ambassadors.

What ambassadors do

Ambassadors attend at least two graduate student events each semester, among them the Graduate Student Welcome, the Grad Slam competition, and the end-of-year celebration. They promote Office of Graduate Studies events and programs on their own social media channels, and they complete a video interview for the Graduate Studies website.

Benefits

Ambassadors and their work are featured on the Office of Graduate Studies website and social media channels. Each ambassador receives a customized Graduate Student Ambassador sweatshirt. Ambassadors also give input to the Graduate Advisory Council, which is made up of the coordinators of every graduate program on campus.

Eligibility

To serve as a Graduate Student Ambassador you must be enrolled in a graduate program at Cal State East Bay, whether master’s, doctoral, credential, or certificate. Your expected graduation date must fall no earlier than the end of your term of service, and you must be available to start early in the semester you’re selected for.

How to apply

Complete the interest survey. Ambassadors are selected and in place by the third week of classes. Questions go to the Office of Graduate Studies at gradstudies@csueastbay.edu.
