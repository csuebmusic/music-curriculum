With the transferred lower-division and general education units from the Associate Degree for Transfer, the degree totals 120 units.

Applied lessons begin after an applied area placement: an audition on the student’s instrument or voice, or a creative portfolio in composition or music production.

String players take Chamber Orchestra (MUS 365) as their Major Performing Ensemble.

02Before the first semester

The B.A. requires three semesters of keyboard musicianship: MUS 118, MUS 119, and MUS 218. Transfer students who haven’t completed the sequence take it at CSU East Bay or meet the requirement by credit by exam.

The B.A. requires MUS 202 Writing About Music. Transfer students who haven’t completed GE Second Composition will need to fulfill that requirement with this course the next time it is offered within its 2-year sequence.

The B.A. requires MUS 381 Essentials of Music Technology. Transfer students who haven’t completed a similar technology course will need to fulfill that requirement with this course during their first semester.
