02The five years

The first three years follow the B.A. roadmap, with certificate courses from the fifth semester. You apply to FAST in the junior year. In the fourth year, you complete the B.A. alongside the first two semesters of the M.A. The fifth year is graduate study and the last 2 certificate courses.

Certificate courses are offered on a two-year cycle, Year A and Year B. From the fifth semester on, each semester below is labeled with its rotation year. Course offerings change from year to year: confirm availability, sequencing, and prerequisites with the Music Education Coordinator and a Music Department advisor before you register.

M.A. emphases are Performance (Classical or Jazz), Choral Conducting, and Composition. The GE courses shown are one typical selection: build your own GE plan with your advisor.

String players take Chamber Orchestra (MUS 365) as their Major Performing Ensemble.
