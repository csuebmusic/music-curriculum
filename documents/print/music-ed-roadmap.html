02How the rotation runs

The certificate courses run on a two-year cycle, Year A and Year B, each with a fall and a spring semester. You can begin in either year. Meet with the Music Education Coordinator to build a schedule that fits your standing and, if you’re in the B.A., your remaining degree work.

In the B.A., certificate courses fill the 12 music elective units, and units beyond those 12 count toward the certificate alone. Course offerings change from year to year: confirm availability and prerequisites with a music advisor before you register.
