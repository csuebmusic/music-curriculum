02The two-year rotation

The certificate courses are offered on a two-year cycle, Year A and Year B, each with a fall and a spring semester. Students can begin in either year and meet with the Music Education Coordinator to build a schedule that fits their standing and, for B.A. students, their remaining degree work.

In the B.A., certificate courses fill the 12 music elective units, and units beyond those 12 count toward the certificate alone. Course offerings change from year to year: students confirm availability and prerequisites with a Music Department advisor before registering.
