02Milestones

Advancement to candidacy is at the end of the first year, after 2 units from MUS 601 or MUS 602, 2 units from MUS 604, MUS 605, or MUS 631, 4 units of Graduate Applied Study, and 2 units of ensemble, with a grade point average of at least 3.0. It must be on record before the final semester.

The capstone (a public recital, conducting performance, or lecture on a composition portfolio, by emphasis) and the comprehensive examination are in the final semester. The examination is a degree requirement that has no units.

2026–2027 cohort

In the 2026–2027 catalog, MUS 603 is 3 units, and MUS 693 is 3 units over three semesters.
