02How the years run

The first three years follow the B.A. roadmap. You apply to FAST in the junior year. The fourth year runs the last of the B.A. alongside the first two semesters of the M.A., and the fifth year is graduate study only.

M.A. emphases are Performance (Classical or Jazz), Choral Conducting, and Composition. The GE courses shown are one typical selection: build your own GE plan with your advisor.
