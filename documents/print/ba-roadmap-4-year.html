02Before you start

Applied lessons begin after an applied area placement, which is an audition in your instrument or voice or a creative portfolio in composition or music production. Ensemble placement happens at the first meeting of each ensemble.

General education paths differ from student to student. The GE courses shown are one typical selection for music majors: build your own GE plan with your advisor.

The Single Subject Matter Preparation Certificate in Music can be added to the B.A. Its roadmap shows where the certificate courses fall.
