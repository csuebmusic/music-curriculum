*Plus up to 6 units for transfer students: 3 for the keyboard sequence (MUS 118, MUS 119, and MUS 218) and 3 for MUS 202 Writing About Music.

The certificate courses count within the B.A. units in residence, with up to 4 units beyond the 60 units in residence. The certificate starts in the first semester in residence and runs one full rotation, with MUS 495 Music Education Practicum in the last semester.

Applied lessons begin after an applied area placement: an audition on the student’s instrument or voice, or a creative portfolio in composition or music production.

String players take Chamber Orchestra (MUS 365) as their Major Performing Ensemble.

02Before the first semester

The B.A. requires three semesters of keyboard musicianship: MUS 118, MUS 119, and MUS 218. Transfer students who haven’t completed the sequence take it at CSU East Bay or meet the requirement by credit by exam.

The B.A. requires MUS 202 Writing About Music. Transfer students who haven’t completed GE Second Composition will need to fulfill that requirement with this course.

The B.A. requires MUS 381 Essentials of Music Technology. Transfer students who haven’t completed a similar technology course will need to fulfill that requirement with this course during their first semester.

This plan is a sample for one transfer record. Before registering, students meet with the Music Education Coordinator and a Music Department advisor to build the version that matches their transcript.

General education paths differ from student to student. The GE courses shown are one typical selection for music majors. Students build their own GE plan with an advisor.
