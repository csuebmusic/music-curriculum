With the transferred lower-division and general education units, the B.A. totals 120 units. The certificate is 31 units: 15 satisfy B.A. requirements, 14 fill free elective units, and 2 are beyond the 60 in residence.

MUS 304 is a B.A. requirement and a certificate course.

02Certificate rotation

Certificate courses are offered on a two-year cycle, Year A and Year B. A Year A semester is a fall A or spring A below, and a Year B semester is a fall B or spring B. This plan is a sample for one transfer record and one rotation year: meet with the Music Education Coordinator and a Music Department advisor to build the version that matches your transcript before you register.

Applied lessons begin after an applied area placement: an audition in your instrument or voice, or a creative portfolio in composition or music production.

The GE courses shown are one typical selection: build your own GE plan with your advisor.

String players take Chamber Orchestra (MUS 365) as their Major Performing Ensemble.

04Notes for transfer students

You need 3 semesters of keyboard musicianship to graduate. If you arrive without them, take MUS 118, MUS 119, and MUS 218 here. If you already have the proficiency, you can meet the requirement by credit by exam.

MUS 202 Writing About Music also satisfies GE Second Composition. If you completed Second Composition before transferring, you don’t take MUS 202. If you didn’t, MUS 202 adds 3 units to the plan.

MUS 381 Essentials of Music Technology is required and isn’t on this plan. If you transfer in an equivalent course, the requirement is met. If you don’t, MUS 381 adds 3 units to the plan.
