With the transferred lower-division and general education units, the B.A. totals 120 units. Blended graduate coursework replaces part of the music electives. MUS 304 is a B.A. requirement and a certificate course.

02Certificate rotation

Certificate courses run on a two-year cycle, Year A and Year B, and each semester below carries its rotation year. If you enter in a Year B fall, each certificate course falls one rotation position later, and the last one comes after the M.A. is complete. Course offerings change from year to year: confirm availability, sequencing, and prerequisites with the Music Education Coordinator and a Music Department advisor before you register.

M.A. emphases are Performance (Classical or Jazz), Choral Conducting, and Composition. The GE courses shown are one typical selection: build your own GE plan with your advisor.

String players take Chamber Orchestra (MUS 365) as their Major Performing Ensemble.

04Notes for transfer students

You need 3 semesters of keyboard musicianship to graduate. If you arrive without them, take MUS 118, MUS 119, and MUS 218 here. A credit-by-exam option is open to students who already have the proficiency.

MUS 202 Writing About Music also satisfies GE Second Composition. If you completed Second Composition before transferring, you don’t take MUS 202. If you didn’t, take it here in place of free elective units.

MUS 381 Essentials of Music Technology is required. If you transfer in an equivalent course, the requirement is met. If you don’t, take MUS 381 here in place of free elective units.
