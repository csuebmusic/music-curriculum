Four things to line up before the semester starts.

  • Applied area placement. Every music major does one before starting lessons on a principal instrument or voice, any time up to the end of the first week. A scholarship audition already counts. What to prepare.
  • Ensembles. Auditions and placement happen at the first meeting of each ensemble. Register for the course, then turn up to that first class. Our ensembles.
  • Advising. You’ll be assigned a faculty advisor in the Department for the major itself. General education runs through Bay Advisor, where your GE advisor and the rest of your success team are listed. See both before you register.
  • Keyboard. Three semesters of group keyboard, normally MUS 118, MUS 119, and MUS 218. Get them into your plan early.

Not sure where to start? Call (510) 885-3135 or write music@csueastbay.edu.
