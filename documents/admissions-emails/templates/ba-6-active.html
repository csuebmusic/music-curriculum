You’re enrolled, and you’re one of ours now.

Two habits to start with. Come to concerts: while you’re enrolled in applied lessons you need ten each semester, any Music Department event counts as one credit, and performing in one counts as a half. Up to two may come from outside the Department, approved in advance. And use the building: the Music Resource Center and the Music Equipment Office have the scores, instruments, and gear you’ll want.

Everything else lives on the Student Resources page: juries, recitals, exams, health and safety, and the policies you’ll want to know before you need them. Concerts and deadlines go up on News & Events.

Your applied teacher and your advisor are your first stops. After that, Call (510) 885-3135 or write music@csueastbay.edu. A person reads that inbox.

All the best,
