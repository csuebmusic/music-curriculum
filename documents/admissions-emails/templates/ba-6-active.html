You are enrolled now, and welcome to the Department.

Read the Undergraduate Handbook when you have an hour. Applied levels and juries, recital requirements, the keyboard sequence, ensembles, health and safety, and the policies you’ll want in hand are all in it. Student Resources is the shorter route, with advising, roadmaps, and forms alongside.

One thing to build into the semester from the start is concert attendance: while you are enrolled in applied lessons you need ten a semester, any Department event counts as one credit, and performing in one counts as a half. You record your own attendance with OneTap on your phone, and the visitor app is worth installing before your first event.

The building is yours to use. The Music Resource Center and the Music Equipment Office hold the scores, instruments, and gear, and concerts and deadlines go up on News & Events.

Your applied teacher and your advisor are the first people to ask. After that, call (510) 885-3135 or write music@csueastbay.edu.

All the best,
