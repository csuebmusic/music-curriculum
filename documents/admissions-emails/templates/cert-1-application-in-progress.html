Thanks for starting an application to the Single Subject Matter Preparation Certificate in Music. It’s the path to the California Single Subject Teaching Credential in Music, which lets you teach any area of music from kindergarten through high school.

This route is for people who already hold a bachelor’s degree.

What goes into CSUApply

  • Your CV or résumé.
  • Contact details for two references. They’ll be asked for letters later. Give them a heads up.
  • A statement of purpose, 2 to 3 double-spaced pages, on your background in music and why you want to teach K–12.
  • Unofficial transcripts from every school you’ve attended.

Questions while you put it together? Call (510) 885-3135 or write music@csueastbay.edu. A person reads that inbox.
