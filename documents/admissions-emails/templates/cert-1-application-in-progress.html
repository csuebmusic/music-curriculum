Thanks for starting an application to the Single Subject Matter Preparation Certificate in Music. The certificate is the subject matter half of becoming a music teacher in California. It establishes that you know the discipline you intend to teach, across theory, history, conducting, and performance, at the depth the state expects of someone standing in front of a classroom. Completing it satisfies the subject matter requirement without a separate examination.

The teaching credential itself is the other half, and it is a separate program run by the School of Education. You apply to it once the subject matter preparation is done. If you came here looking for the credential program, that is where to start.

This route is for people who already hold a bachelor’s degree.

What goes into CSUApply

  • Your CV or résumé.
  • Two letters of recommendation, from people who can speak to your readiness to teach music. You enter their names and email addresses in CSUApply, and each one receives an automatic message with a form to complete and a place to upload the letter. Give them warning before you enter them.
  • A statement of purpose, 2 to 3 double-spaced pages, on your background in music and why you want to teach K–12.
  • Unofficial transcripts from every school you’ve attended.
