Congratulations, and welcome! You have been admitted to the B.A. in Music at Cal State East Bay!

First, accept your offer

Log in to MyCSUEB, choose the Admissions tile, select Accept Admission, and pay the $110 non-refundable pre-enrollment fee, which is applied toward orientation. The Office of Admissions covers the rest of the university side, from housing to student life, on its after you’re accepted page.

To help you decide if CSU East Bay is the right place for you

  • What the four years hold. 120 units built on applied lessons and ensembles from your first semester to your last, alongside theory, history, conducting, and technology. The coursework spans performance, composition, production, jazz, and music education. Our students play across the Bay Area and abroad, write and record their own work, and leave ready for the stage, the studio, the classroom, or graduate school. The roadmaps show the degree term by term, four years or two years for transfer students.
  • The music education add-on. You can earn the Single Subject Matter Preparation Certificate in Music alongside the B.A., which prepares you to apply for the California Single Subject Teaching Credential in Music and complete it successfully. John Eros coordinates music education: john.eros@csueastbay.edu.
  • The FAST 4+1 B.A./M.A. gets you both degrees in five years. You apply by January 15 of your junior year, with 60 to 90 units done and a 3.0 in your music courses. More about the program, and the roadmaps lay out the whole sequence: 4+1 and 2+1 for transfer students.
  • Come and see us. Sit in on a class, hear a rehearsal, meet the faculty you would study with, and talk to the students already doing it. Write or call and we will arrange a visit around your schedule.
  • Complete your applied area placement. You need one to begin lessons in your principal area (if you submitted a scholarship audition, that already counts!) If you have not auditioned, sign up on the scholarship auditions and applied area placements page, any time up to the second week of the semester.
