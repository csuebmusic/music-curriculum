Congratulations, and welcome. You have been admitted to the B.A. in Music at Cal State East Bay!

First, accept your offer

Log in to MyCSUEB, choose the Admissions tile, select Accept Admission, and pay the $110 non-refundable pre-enrollment fee, which is applied toward orientation. Everything below waits on this. The Office of Admissions covers the rest of the university side, from housing to student life, on its after you’re admitted page.

Then, between now and your first week

  • Get your music schedule. Most of this happens between late May and early July. If you are entering as a first-year student, the CLASS Student Success Center builds your first-semester schedule with the department’s music advisors, and you do not register yourself. If you are transferring or coming with a bachelor’s already, we schedule an advising session with you, and you register once your courses are settled.
  • Complete your applied area placement. You need one to begin lessons in your principal area. A scholarship audition already counts. If you have not auditioned, sign up on the scholarship auditions and applied area placements page, any time up to the second week of the semester.
  • Come to music orientation. The Department holds it the day before classes start. You will walk our facilities, rent a locker, and meet your cohort and your advisors. We send the date and details by email over the summer.
