Two years, and they go quickly. The capstone begins in your second semester and runs through the third and fourth, so start thinking about repertoire, program, or portfolio well before you have to. The comprehensive examination is oral, taken in the final semester, and ranges across your area and the seminar core.

Use the building. The Music Resource Center and the Music Equipment Office hold the scores, instruments, and gear. Policies, juries, and deadlines are on the Student Resources page, and concerts go up on News & Events.

Bring anything about sequencing, advancement to candidacy, the capstone, or the exam to the Graduate Coordinator: music@csueastbay.edu, (510) 885-3135.
