You are enrolled now, and welcome.

Read the Graduate Handbook when you have an hour. Sequencing, the Level 6 juries for your area, advancement to candidacy, the capstone, and the comprehensive examination are all in it, and Student Resources carries the same material in shorter form.

The degree runs two years and they go quickly. Advancement to candidacy falls at the end of the first year, and the capstone starts in your second semester and runs through the third and fourth, which leaves room to settle on repertoire, program, or portfolio before you have to. The comprehensive examination is oral, taken in the final semester, and scored in three categories: your area of emphasis, analysis and musical styles, and the profession and its contexts.

The building is yours to use. The Music Resource Center and the Music Equipment Office hold the scores, instruments, and gear, and concerts go up on News & Events.

Anything to do with sequencing, candidacy, the capstone, or the exam comes to the Graduate Coordinator. Write to the Graduate Coordinator at music@csueastbay.edu or call (510) 885-3135.
