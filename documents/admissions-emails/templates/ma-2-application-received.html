We read graduate applications as they come in, and once the Office of Graduate Admissions has been through your CSUApply file we move to a decision. Your status shows up in MyCSUEB.

Paying for it

The Office of Graduate Studies runs fellowships, research funding, and other support across the University: Graduate Opportunities. The Department has its own: Music Scholarships.

  • Graduate Teaching Associates teach. You’d lead classroom or lab sections, build course materials, run exams, tutor, and grade. You need to be admitted to a CSUEB graduate degree program related to the assignment, enrolled, and holding a 3.0 with progress toward the degree. Pay is per course-unit assigned.
  • Instructional Student Assistants support teaching: grading, tutoring, and related work. You need relevant coursework and enrollment in at least 4 units, or recent enrollment with continued eligibility. Up to 20 hours a week, at rates set by level.
  • The Glenn Glasow Graduate Fellowship in Composition covers a year of study for one composer, who premieres a new piece at the Glenn Glasow Memorial Concert. There is no separate application. We choose from the scores already in your program application.
