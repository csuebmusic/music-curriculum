Congratulations, your application to the Single Subject Matter Preparation Certificate in Music is complete.

We read the materials you sent us, your CV, your letters, and the evidence of your abilities, as they come in. The Office of Graduate Admissions verifies your transcripts and official documents separately, and we move to a decision once both are done. You can track your status in MyCSUEB.

Two things worth repeating

  • This is the subject matter certificate, not the credential. It establishes that you know the discipline you intend to teach. The teaching credential is a separate program run by the School of Education.
  • The stand-alone certificate carries no federal or state financial aid. Aid eligibility follows admission to a bachelor’s, master’s, or credential program. If you need aid, apply to the B.A. in Music or the M.A. in Music and take the certificate alongside it. Write to the Graduate Coordinator and she will go through which one fits.

Questions about the coursework or the credential pathway go to John Eros, who coordinates music education, at john.eros@csueastbay.edu. Anything about admission or sequencing goes to Inés Thiebaut, our Graduate Coordinator, at ines.thiebaut@csueastbay.edu or (510) 885-3135.
