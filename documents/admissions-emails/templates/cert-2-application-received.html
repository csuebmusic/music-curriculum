Congratulations, your application to the Single Subject Matter Preparation Certificate in Music is complete.

We read the materials you sent us, your CV, your letters, and the evidence of your abilities, as they come in. The Office of Graduate Admissions verifies your transcripts and official documents separately, and we move to a decision once both are done. You can track your status in MyCSUEB.

Important financial information

The stand-alone certificate is not eligible for federal or state financial aid. The University’s Program Participation Agreement does not cover certificate programs at any level, undergraduate or graduate, and aid eligibility follows admission to a bachelor’s, master’s, or credential program.

If you need aid, the route is to apply to a degree and take the certificate alongside it. Without a bachelor’s degree, or with one in a field other than music, that means the B.A. in Music. With a bachelor’s in music already, it means the M.A. in Music. Write to the Graduate Coordinator before you decide and she will go through which one fits your situation.

What this certificate is, and what it is not

This is a subject matter preparation certificate. It is not the teaching credential. The credential is a separate program run by the School of Education.

Teaching opportunities

The graduate program offers teaching opportunities to its graduate students as need arises. Let the Graduate Coordinator know if you are interested.
