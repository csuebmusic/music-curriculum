To help you decide if CSU East Bay is the right place for you

  • What the certificate covers. The subject matter the state requires for the Single Subject Teaching Credential in Music: theory, history, conducting, and performance, at the depth expected of someone teaching the discipline. Completing it satisfies the subject matter requirement without a separate examination.
  • What it is not. The teaching credential itself is a separate program run by the School of Education.
  • Aid, if you need it. The stand-alone certificate is not eligible for federal or state financial aid. If that matters to your plans, apply to the B.A. in Music or the M.A. in Music and take the certificate alongside the degree. Talk to us before you decide.
  • Come and see us. Sit in on a class, hear a rehearsal, and meet the faculty you would work with. Write to John Eros or the Graduate Coordinator and we will arrange a visit around your schedule.

Check in with the offices that handle the rest

  • The Office of Graduate Studies oversees graduate study across the University, and its admitted students page carries orientation, registration, and the next steps after you accept.
  • The Center for International Education advises international students on immigration, arrival, and the mandatory check-in: cie@csueastbay.edu, (510) 885-2880.
