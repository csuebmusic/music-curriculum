Congratulations, and welcome. You have been admitted to the Master of Arts in Music at Cal State East Bay!

First, accept your offer

Log in to MyCSUEB, choose the Admissions tile, and select Accept Admission. The Office of Admissions covers the rest of the university side, from housing to student life, on its after you’re accepted page.

Then, between now and your first semester

  • Know what you are taking on. 32 units over four semesters: a shared seminar core in analysis and post-tonal practice, the history and theory of jazz, interdisciplinary collaboration, advanced conducting, entrepreneurship in the arts, the social and ecological dimensions of music, and teaching music in higher education.
  • Alongside the seminars. Four semesters of applied lessons and ensemble in your area, a public capstone, and an oral comprehensive examination in the final semester.
  • Say early if you want to teach. Teaching Associate and Instructional Student Assistant assignments open before the semester. Tell the Graduate Coordinator if either interests you.

Read ahead

The Graduate Handbook sets out sequencing, the Level 6 juries for your area, advancement to candidacy, the capstone, and the comprehensive examination. The Office of Graduate Studies handles the university side of graduate study.

Whatever comes up, write to Inés Thiebaut, our Graduate Coordinator, at ines.thiebaut@csueastbay.edu or call (510) 885-3135. She would rather hear from you early than have you work it out alone. The Office of Graduate Studies handles the university side of graduate study.
