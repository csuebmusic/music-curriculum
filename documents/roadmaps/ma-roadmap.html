Each semester has 8 units. Students declare one area of emphasis at admission: Performance (Classical or Jazz), Choral Conducting, or Composition. The graduate seminars are common to all three emphases.

note

The comprehensive examination is a degree requirement that has no units. It is in the final semester, with the capstone.

Seminars are offered on a rotation. Take them as scheduled, in consultation with the Graduate Coordinator.

milestones

Advancement to candidacy is at the end of the first year, after 2 units from MUS 601 or MUS 602, 2 units from MUS 604, MUS 605, or MUS 631, 4 units of applied music, and 2 units of ensemble, with a grade point average of at least 3.0. It must be on record before the final semester. The requirements are in the Graduate Handbook.

The capstone (a public recital, conducting performance, or lecture on a composition portfolio, by emphasis) and the comprehensive examination are in the final semester. Both are described in section 03 of the Graduate Handbook.

fast and the certificate

Students admitted through FAST follow the FAST 4+1 roadmap or the FAST 2+1 transfer roadmap. On those roadmaps, the first two graduate semesters are taken alongside the last two of the B.A.

M.A. students can add the Single Subject Matter Preparation Certificate in Music. Its courses and their two-year rotation are in the certificate rotation.
