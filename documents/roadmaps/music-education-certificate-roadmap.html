the two-year rotation

The certificate courses are offered on a two-year cycle. Year A and Year B each have a fall and a spring semester, and the cycle repeats.

Year A is fall A and spring A, and Year B is fall B and spring B. Students can begin in either year and meet with John Eros, the Music Education Coordinator, to build a schedule that fits their standing and remaining B.A. work.

before registering

Course offerings change from year to year. Students confirm semester availability, sequencing, and prerequisites with a Music Department advisor before registering.

courses that double count

Eight certificate courses may also fulfill Music Elective requirements in the B.A. in Music:

  • MUS 331 Foundations of Music Education
  • MUS 335 Technology for Music Educators
  • MUS 341 Basic Conducting
  • MUS 431 Elementary Music Teaching Methods
  • MUS 432 Instrumental Music Teaching Methods
  • MUS 433 Choral Music Teaching Methods
  • MUS 441 Intermediate Conducting
  • MUS 495 Music Education Practicum

MUS 304 World Music & Culture counts as a required B.A. course and as a certificate course.
