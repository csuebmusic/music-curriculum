how the rotation runs

The certificate courses run on a two-year cycle. Year A and Year B each carry a fall and a spring semester, and the cycle repeats.

Students may begin in either Year A or Year B. Meet with Dr. Eros to build a schedule that fits your standing and your remaining B.A. work. MUS 304 may be taken whenever it is offered.

before you register

Course offerings change from year to year. Confirm semester availability, sequencing, and prerequisites with a Music Department advisor before you register.

courses that double count

Seven certificate courses may also fulfill Music Elective requirements in the B.A. in Music:

  • MUS 331 Foundations of Music Education
  • MUS 335 Technology for Music Educators
  • MUS 341 Basic Conducting
  • MUS 431 Elementary Music Teaching Methods
  • MUS 432 Instrumental Music Teaching Methods
  • MUS 433 Choral Music Teaching Methods
  • MUS 441 Intermediate Conducting

MUS 304 World Music and Culture counts as a required B.A. course and as a certificate course. Certificate students may substitute MUS 335 for the required MUS 381 Essentials of Music Technology.
