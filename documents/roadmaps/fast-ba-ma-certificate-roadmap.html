Blended graduate coursework stands in for part of the music electives.

Seven certificate courses also fulfill music electives: MUS 331, MUS 335, MUS 341, MUS 431, MUS 432, MUS 433, MUS 441. MUS 304 is a required B.A. course and a certificate course. MUS 335 substitutes for MUS 381.

note

The seventh and eighth semesters run the last of the B.A. alongside the first of the M.A. Rows marked blended count toward both degrees. Applied study, ensemble, and the capstone sequence continue through the fifth year. Certificate coursework runs from the third year through the fifth, and the certificate is complete at the end of the tenth semester.

General education paths differ from student to student. The GE courses shown are a typical selection for music majors, not a fixed sequence. Build your own GE plan with your advisor.

certificate rotation

Certificate courses run on a two-year cycle of Year A and Year B semesters. The semesters below carry the same labels: the fifth and ninth semesters are Year A falls, the sixth and tenth are Year A springs, the seventh is a Year B fall, and the eighth is a Year B spring. Each certificate course appears in the semester its rotation slot allows.

before you register

Course offerings change from year to year. Confirm semester availability, sequencing, and prerequisites with Dr. Eros and with a Music Department advisor before you register.
