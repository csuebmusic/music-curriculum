notes for transfer students

This plan covers the upper-division B.A. in Music, the M.A. in Music, and the Music Education Certificate completed in residence at CSU East Bay. Students arrive at junior standing with the lower-division and general-education work behind them. For how completed coursework transfers to CSUEB, see transfer admission information.

before you start

Class piano. You need 3 semesters of keyboard musicianship to graduate. Transfer students sometimes arrive without it. We strongly encourage completing the sequence with us: MUS 118, MUS 119, and MUS 218. A credit-by-exam option is available for students who already have the proficiency.

MUS 202 Writing About Music. This required course also satisfies the GE Second Composition requirement, and it does not appear on the plan below. If you completed Second Composition before transferring, as most Associate Degree for Transfer students have, you are not required to take MUS 202. If you did not, you complete it here, drawing on the free elective units in the plan below.

MUS 381 Essentials of Music Technology. Certificate students meet this requirement with MUS 335 Technology for Music Educators, which appears in the fourth semester below.

With the transferred lower-division and general-education units, the B.A. totals 120 units.

Blended graduate coursework stands in for part of the music electives.

Seven certificate courses also fulfill music electives: MUS 331, MUS 335, MUS 341, MUS 431, MUS 432, MUS 433, MUS 441. MUS 304 is a required B.A. course and a certificate course. MUS 335 substitutes for MUS 381.

note

The third and fourth semesters run the last of the B.A. alongside the first of the M.A. Rows marked blended count toward both degrees. Applied study, ensemble, and the capstone sequence continue through the third year. Certificate coursework runs from the first semester in residence through the sixth, and the certificate is complete at the end of the sixth.

General education paths differ from student to student. The GE courses shown are a typical selection for music majors, not a fixed sequence. Build your own GE plan with your advisor.

certificate rotation

Certificate courses run on a two-year cycle of Year A and Year B semesters. The semesters below carry the same labels: the first and fifth are Year A falls, the second and sixth are Year A springs, the third is a Year B fall, and the fourth is a Year B spring. Each certificate course appears in the semester its rotation slot allows. A student entering on a Year B fall follows the same courses one rotation position later, with the last certificate course falling after the M.A. is complete.
