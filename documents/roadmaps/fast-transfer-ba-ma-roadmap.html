notes for transfer students

This plan covers the upper-division B.A. in Music and the M.A. in Music completed in residence at CSU East Bay. Students enter at junior standing with lower-division and general education coursework complete. For how completed coursework transfers to CSU East Bay, see transfer admission information.

before the first semester

The B.A. requires three semesters of keyboard musicianship: MUS 118, MUS 119, and MUS 218. Transfer students who haven’t completed the sequence take it at CSU East Bay or meet the requirement by credit by exam.

The B.A. requires MUS 202 Writing About Music. Transfer students who haven’t completed GE Second Composition will need to fulfill that requirement with this course the next time it is offered within its 2-year sequence.

The B.A. requires MUS 381 Essentials of Music Technology. Transfer students who haven’t completed a similar technology course will need to fulfill that requirement with this course during their first semester.

admission to FAST

Transfer students apply to FAST by the end of week 7 of the second semester in residence and begin FAST coursework the following semester. Eligibility, the application, and the rules after admission are in section 08 of the Undergraduate Handbook.

note

The third and fourth semesters in residence complete the B.A. alongside the first two semesters of the M.A. Rows marked blended count toward both degrees, and rows marked graduate credit by petition count toward the M.A. only.

General education paths differ from student to student. The GE courses shown are one typical selection for music majors. Students build their own GE plan with an advisor.

entry term

Department courses are offered on a two-year rotation of fall A, spring A, fall B, and spring B. Fall A is in even years (2026, 2028, and so on), and fall B is in odd years (2027, 2029, and so on). Each entry term has its own plan.
