# roadmaps

Student-facing HTML roadmaps for the B.A. in Music, house-styled on
`../handbooks/handbooks.css`. Generated from the department's 2026-2027
roadmap PDFs and intended as the editable, linkable home for that content.

- `ba-roadmap-4-year.html`: first-year entry, 120 units.
- `ba-roadmap-2-year-transfer.html`: Music ADT transfer entry, 60 units in residence.
- `music-education-certificate-roadmap.html`: certificate course rotation across a two-year cycle, 31 units.
- `music-education-certificate-ba-sequence.html`: the certificate as sequenced in the B.A. roadmap, semesters 5 to 8.

Keep course titles and units in sync with the University Catalog and the
undergraduate handbook (`../handbooks/undergraduate-handbook.html`). Each
semester carries an anchored id (`sem1` to `sem8`) for deep linking.

`roadmaps.css` layers one cool secondary color over the handbook style for
general-education rows. It applies to the roadmaps alone: the handbooks keep
the single-accent house style.
