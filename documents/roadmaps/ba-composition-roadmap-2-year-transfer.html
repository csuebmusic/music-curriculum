notes for transfer students

This plan covers the upper-division B.A. in Music completed in residence at CSU East Bay. Students enter at junior standing with lower-division coursework and lower-division general education complete. Transfer admission information explains how completed coursework transfers to CSU East Bay.

before the first semester

The B.A. requires three semesters of keyboard musicianship: MUS 118, MUS 119, and MUS 218. Transfer students who haven’t completed the sequence take it at CSU East Bay or meet the requirement by credit by exam.

The B.A. requires MUS 202 Writing About Music. Transfer students who haven’t completed GE Second Composition will need to fulfill that requirement with this course.

The B.A. requires MUS 381 Essentials of Music Technology. Transfer students who haven’t completed a similar technology course will need to fulfill that requirement with this course during their first semester.

Certificate in Music Education (add-on)

The Certificate in Music Education counts within the B.A. units in residence, with up to 4 units beyond the 60. Its semester placement is in the B.A. in Music with the Certificate in Music Education, 2-year transfer roadmap, and its course rotation is in the Certificate in Music Education roadmap.

Up to 4 additional units of Applied Music Study (MUS 261–269, MUS 461–469) beyond the 4 required may count toward electives. Up to 4 additional units of Major Performing Ensembles (MUS 351–359, or Chamber Orchestra, MUS 365, for string players) beyond the 4 required may count toward electives:

  • East Bay Singers (MUS 351B)
  • East Bay Jazz Orchestra (MUS 355)
  • East Bay Wind Symphony (MUS 353)
  • Chamber Orchestra (MUS 365), for string players

Up to 7 additional units of Chamber Ensembles (MUS 360–369) beyond the 1 required may count toward electives:

  • Rock Band (MUS 360)
  • Chamber Ensemble (MUS 361)
  • Percussion Ensemble (MUS 363)
  • Chamber Orchestra (MUS 365)
  • Electronic Music Ensemble (MUS 366)
  • Piano Ensemble (MUS 367)
  • Jazz Combo (MUS 368)
  • Vocal Repertory Ensemble (MUS 369A)
  • Chamber Singers (MUS 369B)
  • East Bay Jazz Singers (MUS 369C)
  • A Cappella Ensemble (MUS 369D)
