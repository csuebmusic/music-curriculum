notes for transfer students

This plan covers the B.A. in Theatre Arts completed in residence at CSU East Bay. Students enter at junior standing with lower-division general education complete. Transfer admission information explains how completed coursework transfers to CSU East Bay.

core and ensembles

The core includes two lower-division courses, THEA 101 and THEA 254. An advisor reviews AA-T coursework for courses that meet them.

The core repeats three ensembles: DANC 360 three times, DANC 370 three times, and MUS 363B twice, at 1 unit each. Transfer students take two ensembles in most semesters.

DANC 360 is taken with THEA 381 or THEA 382 in the same semester. The 3 units of production support count toward the major electives.

THEA 493 Theatre Capstone is taken in the senior year, with THEA 381, THEA 382, or THEA 360 in the same semester.

major electives

Students complete 9 units of coursework with the prefix ART, COMM, DANC, MUS, or THEA, chosen with the major advisor. Courses used for the core don’t count toward electives, except units of DANC 360, DANC 370, and MUS 363B beyond those the core requires.

DANC 360 is taken with THEA 381 or THEA 382 in the same semester. The 3 units of production support count toward the major electives.
