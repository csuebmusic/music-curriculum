# Curriculum

Course and program proposals and curriculum documentation for the Department of Music and Performing Arts, organized into subsections. Each subsection carries its own README.

## Subsections

- [undergraduate-theory-musicianship](undergraduate-theory-musicianship/): the Theory and Musicianship sequence leading to the B.A. in Music, three framework documents and the MUS 108 to MUS 410 syllabi.
- [catalog-audit](catalog-audit/): audit of the MUS course inventory in the 2026-2027 catalog against the Curriculog proposal archive, and the proposal queue for the 2027-2028 catalog.
- [schedule](schedule/): the course rotation and scheduling rules.
- [ma](ma/): curriculum map and long-term assessment plan for the M.A. in Music, draft for faculty review.
- [theatre-arts-ba](theatre-arts-ba/): curriculum map and long-term assessment plan for the B.A. in Theatre Arts, draft for faculty review.
