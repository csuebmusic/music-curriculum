## launch deadlines

The launch deadline is the date faculty submit a proposal in Curriculog. A proposal is ready for committee review about two weeks after submission. A proposal submitted on the final deadline instead of the launch deadline doesn't move forward in time.

The committee has five to seven members: the CLASS associate dean and, where possible, one probationary or tenured faculty member from each of the arts, humanities and letters, social sciences, interdisciplinary programs, and professional programs, selected by that group's department and program chairs. Terms are two years. A seat with no faculty member available in its constituency can go to a faculty member from another constituency or stay vacant for the term.

Communication in and around the committee is cordial and professional. A member or guest who departs from this may be excused, with required follow-up meetings with the Associate Dean and the Chair.

## campus deadlines

College deadlines are earlier than these. The CLASS meeting dates above set the college step.

| Date | Deadline | Applies to |
| --- | --- | --- |
| September 1 | Academic Master Plan to the Senate Office for CAPR, submitted by the Dean or Associate Dean | New degree programs, which need Board of Trustees approval before a proposal |
| September 15 | Launch. College committee approval received in APS by October 15 | All course proposals: new, revised, GEOC, non-GEOC, overlay, G.R., UWR, bank, unbank. New prefix requests |
| October 1 | Annual program reviews to the Senate Office for CAPR, submitted by the Dean or Associate Dean | |
| October 15 | Launch. College committee approval received in APS by November 15 | Program revisions that don't change total degree or concentration units. Revisions needing campus and Chancellor's Office approval: degree or concentration title, total units, self-support or state offering, delivery format, discontinuance or suspension, a blended 4+1 program |
| November 15 | Launch. College committee approval received in APS by December 15 | New minors and minor revisions. Formation, dissolution, name change, or merger of a department or academic unit |
| December 1 | Launch. College committee approval received in APS by February 1 | New degree programs, concentrations, credentials, certificates, and subject matter preparation programs. Elevation of a concentration to a degree |
| April 1 | Non-curricular catalog edits to APS, submitted by staff and chairs | |
| May 15 | Final long-term program reviews to the Senate Office for CAPR | |

Course proposals enter the next catalog and class schedule only with Academic Senate approval by February 1. Program proposals launched October 15 need Academic Senate approval by the end of the spring semester. Program revisions and discontinuances take effect only in a fall semester.
