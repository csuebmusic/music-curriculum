# geoc alignment form

Status as of September 28, 2026.

## status

The 2026/2027 GEOC alignment form ([reference/geoc-alignment-form-2026-2027.docx](../reference/geoc-alignment-form-2026-2027.docx)) is a required Curriculog attachment for all new courses, revisions, and recertifications as of Fall 2026.

The proposed short version is [papers/geoc-alignment-form-proposed.docx](../papers/geoc-alignment-form-proposed.docx). Inés sent it to Jennifer Zenovich (GE Director, chair of the GEOC Subcommittee) on September 2, cc Danika LeDuc and Nicholas Baham.

Jennifer replied on September 3:

- She likes the proposed form.
- She won't change the form before the September 15 deadline, because some proposals were already launched with the existing form.
- The existing form was a decision of the previous GEOC, made to streamline approval and recertification.
- Kevin, the previous GE Director, communicated the form to all chairs and received no complaints about its structure.
- The release time concern is valid.
- She'll discuss it with Danika and reply.

Inés replied after that (the date isn't recorded here), asking GEOC to do one of two things:

- accept either form this cycle, with proposals already started continuing on the existing form and new ones using the short version, or
- take the short form, or one like it, to GEOC for the next cycle.

Jennifer hasn't replied as of September 28.

## proposed short form

The short form has:

- proposal type (check boxes)
- course name and number
- the areas the proposal is for (check boxes for lower division, upper division, overlay, code, and writing)
- originator
- one syllabus confirmation (check box) covering the catalog description, course and GEOC learning outcomes, a course outline with due dates for key assignments, and word counts where an area sets a writing requirement
- an alignment table with one row per GEOC learning outcome, giving the weeks covered, what students do in those weeks, and the assignment where students demonstrate the outcome and how it's evaluated
- a course characteristics table giving each characteristic and how and where the syllabus meets it
- a delivery methods box

It drops the week-by-week grid and the full assignment list from the existing form. Both are already on the syllabus under the syllabus policy.

## committee letter

Jessica Santone reported to the committee, before the September 29 meeting, that the CLASS Council of Chairs recommended the CCC send an official response to the alignment form. She wants the committee to write it this year and doesn't expect there to be time this week.

Her concerns:

- The form adds to reviewer workload.
- Its granularity lengthens syllabi. She recalls that the earlier syllabus policy, from her CIC service in 2020–2022, aimed to shorten syllabi.

The GEOC checklist requires word counts for all written assignments on the syllabus. The syllabus policy doesn't ([reference/currency.md](../reference/currency.md)).
