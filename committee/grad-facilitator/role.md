# graduate recruitment facilitator

Appointment accepted August 2026 for a two-year term, carrying a stipend. Added to existing duties (Associate Professor, Department of Music; Graduate Coordinator; composition and music production area coordinator; CLASS Curriculum Committee chair), with no reduction in any of them.

The role sits in the Office of Graduate Studies and has no standing committee. The work is ongoing rather than tied to set deliverable dates. Promotion and recruitment across the graduate programs is this position's portfolio; the other Graduate Studies facilitators cover other areas.

## description

Coordinate, develop, and lead all aspects of graduate program promotion and recruitment.

- Promote graduate program opportunities across campus and externally: information sessions, email campaigns, class visits.
- Work with the CSU East Bay Recruitment and Marketing team to develop and implement strategies to increase graduate program applications and yield.
- Support departments in identifying and recruiting qualified students.
- Configure messaging to prospective students in Salesforce Marketing Cloud.
- Attend graduate school fairs at other universities to promote East Bay graduate programs.

## scope

Every graduate program, at equal priority. The Office of Graduate Studies page carries the canonical list: 31 master's programs, the Ed.D. in Educational Leadership, eight credentials, eight certificates, and the FAST accelerated tracks.

Separate from the Music Department graduate coordinator role, which covers the M.A. in Music alone. Recruitment work for the M.A. in Music runs through this charge like any other program.
