## GAC recruitment plan, November 2024

Seven areas, with the action steps as written.

### 1. define the target audience

Student types: traditional (current and recent undergraduates), mid-career professionals seeking advancement, international students, non-traditional students returning after a gap or balancing work and study.

Action steps: determine how each group is best reached; review current enrollment data for student origin. Feeder schools by program are on the Pioneer Insights graduate enrollment dashboard, Enrollment by Origin Institute tab.

### 2. strengthen online presence

Website: complete admission requirements and deadlines, user-friendly navigation, a clear call to action with Request More Info and Apply Here buttons, student testimonials, faculty highlights, success stories.

Search and content: optimize for program search terms; publish research highlights and faculty Q&A.

Social media: presence on LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook, and X; program details, alumni stories, student life, faculty interviews; respond to comments and messages.

Benchmark: Student Outreach recommends two to three posts a week. Alternatives to carrying that load at program level are submitting posts to the college account or to Graduate Studies, and asking current students to post from their own accounts. University digital marketing campaign materials are on Google Drive, for alignment with university-wide efforts.

### 3. host virtual information sessions and webinars

Monthly or twice-a-semester virtual sessions with faculty and staff covering program details, questions, and application procedure. Simple registration page. Alumni and current students as speakers. Topic-specific webinars, for example navigating the application process. Promotion by email and social media, cross-promoted through student organizations, faculty newsletters, and department pages.

Benchmark: programs that hold sessions report strong interest with little external marketing. Sign-up link, contact information, date, and time need to be easy to find on the program website. The MBA program recommends walking participants through the website live rather than presenting slides. Graduate Studies is building an index of information sessions on its site and will post them on its social channels.

### 4. partner with external organizations and networks

Industry and professional associations, with exclusive webinars or workshops for their members. Alumni promotion through social media, info-session appearances, and referrals; an alumni ambassador program. Industry advisory boards as recruiting channels through their internal communications.

Benchmark: co-hosted sessions, webinars, and panels reach new audiences when the event carries value for the partner organization, for example access to program resources, faculty exposure, or training materials for their members.

### 5. nurture leads and maintain relationships

Automated follow-up email to prospects after an information session registration or a brochure download, with admissions detail and deadlines. Personal outreach from staff or faculty to highly engaged leads. A step-by-step application guide or checklist, and webinars on preparing application materials.

Benchmark: overall yield across all East Bay programs stands at 9 percent. The former MBA program coordinator called every information-session attendee individually and reported a 30 percent yield. Admissions is building a Graduate Admissions Portal serving personalized information to students who have filed a Request for Information or started a Cal State Apply application, with personalized messaging to move them to completion or acceptance. Departments can message their own leads through Salesforce Marketing Cloud; ITS holds access.

### 6. track metrics and optimize

KPIs: webinar attendance, lead conversion rate, application submission rate, and qualified leads by channel. Pre-registration for events makes channel attribution straightforward. Funnel analysis for drop-off points, for example webinar registration that does not convert to application. Post-session feedback from attendees.

### 7. provide financial and career support information

Scholarships, assistantships, and fellowships stated plainly, with new master's students directed to apply for Graduate Equity Fellowships on acceptance. Career Empowerment Center promotion, with any company partnerships, internships, and job placement programs named.
