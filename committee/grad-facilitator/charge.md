# graduate recruitment facilitator

Appointment accepted August 2026. Added to existing duties (Associate Professor, Department of Music; Graduate Coordinator; composition and music production area coordinator; CLASS Curriculum Committee chair), with no reduction in any of them.

## charge

Coordinate, develop, and lead all aspects of graduate program promotion and recruitment.

- Promote graduate program opportunities across campus and externally: information sessions, email campaigns, class visits.
- Work with the CSU East Bay Recruitment and Marketing team to develop and implement strategies to increase graduate program applications and yield.
- Support departments in identifying and recruiting qualified students.
- Configure messaging to prospective students in Salesforce Marketing Cloud.
- Attend graduate school fairs at other universities to promote East Bay graduate programs.

## scope

University-wide, across all graduate programs. Separate from the Music Department graduate coordinator role, which covers the M.A. in Music alone. Recruitment work for the M.A. in Music runs through this charge like any other program.

## added at intake

Raised by Kevin in the initial discussion and carried as part of the opening work:

- Meet with department chairs and associate deans about outreach and advertisement.
- Meet with the College of Business and Economics about outreach and marketing strategies transferable to other programs.
