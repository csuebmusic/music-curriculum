# enrollment baseline

From Pioneer Insights exports pulled August 2026: graduate enrollment by college and program and enrollment by prior institution, both Fall 2021 through Fall 2025, and a degrees and plans table carrying Fall 2025 against Fall 2026.

The dashboard export is marked all funding sources and totals 2,144 for Fall 2025. The degrees and plans table totals 1,743 for the same term. Twenty-five programs carry the same Fall 2025 figure in both and are combined into a single series below; the rest are held out.

On the dashboard basis, down 586 students, 21 percent, from Fall 2021 to Fall 2025.

The two columns are not comparable. For Fall 2025 they differ by 401 students, the programs and funding sources the plans table leaves out. A Fall 2026 figure on the dashboard basis is not in the exports supplied, so the four-year decline cannot yet be extended to Fall 2026.
