# enrollment baseline

From Pioneer Insights exports pulled August 2026: graduate enrollment by college and program and enrollment by prior institution, both Fall 2021 through Fall 2025, and a degrees and plans table carrying Fall 2025 against Fall 2026.

Read the two sources separately. They do not cover the same set of programs. The dashboard export is marked all funding sources and totals 2,144 for Fall 2025. The degrees and plans table totals 1,743 for the same term and omits Accountancy, Nursing, Reading and Literacy, Quantitative Economics, and Marine Science, so it is not a full census.

| term | headcount |

Down 586 students, 21 percent, across four years.
