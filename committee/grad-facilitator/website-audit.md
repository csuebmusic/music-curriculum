# graduate studies website audit

Working file for the Office of Graduate Studies section of the public site: what it holds, where the same information sits in more than one place, and the consolidation options open to Graduate Studies.

Crawled September 14, 2026 from `https://www.csueastbay.edu/graduate-studies/`, 30 HTML pages and 35 files. Page-level results in `documents/grad-site-inventory.tsv`, program names by page in `documents/grad-site-overlap.tsv`, both regenerated by `.github/workflows/grad-site-inventory.yml`. Tile destinations in `documents/tile-link-check.tsv`. Program roster and the tile and catalog discrepancies in `programs.md`.

## program listings

Three live listings of the same programs sit inside the section.

| listing | form | count |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [landing page tiles](https://www.csueastbay.edu/graduate-studies/) | image tiles in four groups, each linking to a department page | 46 |
| [graduate program coordinators](https://www.csueastbay.edu/graduate-studies/graduate-program-coordinators.html) | two sections, master's and doctoral, then credential and certificate; about 100 links out to departments | 784 words |
| [prospective graduate students](https://www.csueastbay.edu/graduate-studies/prospective-grad-students.html) | department list grouped by college, under Admission Requirements | 1,119 words |

The retired graduate program directory was a fourth until it came down on or before September 14, 2026.

Each is edited separately. The coordinators page is built for advising and carries the coordinator contact rather than program information.

## repeated pages

Grad Slam has four pages, all four in the left navigation: `grad-slam-competition.html` at 1,455 words, and 2025, 2024, and 2023 at 1,392, 1,298, and 1,201.

FAST has two pages, `fast-programs.html` at 1,870 words and `fast-program-details.html` at 9,641, and 24 pairing sheets in `files/docs/fast-catalog/`, most pairings carrying both a 25-26 and a 26-27 file with the old year live.

The graduate student handbook runs to six pages. Four are under 300 words: introduction 269, admission and enrollment 519, additional information 111, contact information 129. The requirements page is 6,931.

## contact information

`gradstudies@csueastbay.edu` appears on ten pages. The landing page carries the full staff block with personal addresses for the AVP, the Faculty Director, and four facilitators.

## the landing page at three paths

`/graduate-studies/`, `/gradstudies/index.html`, and `/aps/graduate-studies/index.html` are all indexed with the landing page content, and the indexed copies name different Faculty Directors. The two alternates return a redirect loop to an automated request and have not been opened in a browser.

## consolidation options

Four category tiles. The landing page carries one tile each for master's, doctoral, credential, and certificate, each going to a single list page. The per-program links leave the landing page.

Single list on the coordinators page. Each row carries program name, department page link, catalog link, and coordinator. The landing page and the prospective page point to it, and the program tiles come down. Settle first whether a prospective student should arrive at a coordinator table, or whether the single list belongs on a new graduate programs page with the coordinator as one column.

Beyond the program listings: Grad Slam to one page with the past years as an archive section, FAST to one page with current-year sheets only, the four short handbook pages folded into the requirements page, one contact page that the others link to, and the alternate landing paths retired or redirected.
