# graduate recruitment facilitator

Working documents for the graduate recruitment facilitator role in the Office of Graduate Studies, held by Inés Thiebaut.

## files

`role.md`, `status.md`, and `programs.md` record current state. `recruitment-plan.md` is the 2024 plan with current notes and proposed actions. Open questions and next actions are in `status.md`. Resolved items come out.

- [role.md](role.md): the appointment, the charge, the scope, and the office contacts.
- [status.md](status.md): the Graduate Studies website status, PR strategies, program status, next actions, and open questions.
- [recruitment-plan.md](recruitment-plan.md): the Graduate Advisory Council (GAC) Consultative Committee recruitment plan of November 11, 2024, verbatim, with notes and proposed actions inline.
- [programs.md](programs.md): the graduate program roster from the Graduate Programs & Coordinators page, the discrepancies against Cal State Apply, and the programs not accepting applications.
